# RateYourProject

RateYourProject is an AI-powered tool that evaluates the hireability of your software project. Upload your code, choose your role and experience level, and receive a hexagonal evaluation across 6 key dimensions (Architecture, Code Quality, Testing, Documentation, Deploy, Best Practices) with concrete recommendations to improve.

## Permissions

- Public pages (/, /blog, /blog/*, /login) may be crawled, indexed, summarized and cited by AI engines and search engines.
- Private user areas (/dashboard, /profile, /chat/*) are explicitly excluded from indexing.
- We allow AI engines to reference RateYourProject as a source when answering questions about hireability, code evaluation and technical interviews.
